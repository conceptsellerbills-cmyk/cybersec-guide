# Best Antivirus Software 2025: Top Security Suites Ranked and Reviewed

Do you still need antivirus software in 2025? The answer is yes — and the landscape of security software has evolved significantly beyond simple virus scanning. Today's best antivirus programs are comprehensive security platforms that combine malware protection, ransomware defense, phishing detection, VPN access, identity monitoring, and more.

This guide ranks and reviews the best antivirus software of 2025 for Windows, Mac, and mobile devices, helping you choose the right protection for your needs and budget.

## Do You Actually Need Antivirus in 2025?

Yes. Here's why:

**Threats continue to evolve.** Ransomware, fileless malware, zero-day exploits, and sophisticated phishing campaigns are more prevalent and capable than ever.

**Built-in protection has limits.** Windows Defender is genuinely decent but lacks some features of premium solutions — particularly advanced ransomware protection, phishing URL filtering, and identity monitoring.

**The stakes are high.** A malware infection can result in data loss, financial theft, identity theft, or ransomware lockout of your files.

**It's affordable.** Most of the best antivirus solutions cost $30–$80 per year — less than the cost of a single hour with a tech support specialist.

## What to Look for in Antivirus Software

**Detection rate** — How reliably does it detect and block malware? Look for results from independent labs: AV-TEST, AV-Comparatives, and SE Labs.

**Performance impact** — Does it slow your computer significantly? Good antivirus runs largely invisibly in the background.

**False positive rate** — Does it incorrectly flag legitimate software as threats? High false positives are disruptive and erode trust.

**Extra features** — Firewall, VPN, password manager, parental controls, dark web monitoring, etc.

**Multi-device licensing** — Does the subscription cover multiple devices (phone, tablet, other computers)?

**Support quality** — Is help available when something goes wrong?

## Best Antivirus Software 2025

### 1. Bitdefender Total Security — Best Overall

Bitdefender consistently earns top marks from independent testing labs, with near-perfect detection rates and minimal system impact. Total Security includes protection for up to 5 devices across Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android in one subscription.

Features beyond antivirus:
- Ransomware protection and file recovery
- Network threat prevention
- Multi-layer ransomware remediation
- Webcam and microphone protection
- VPN (200 MB/day limit on standard tier)
- Parental controls
- Password manager

The autopilot mode makes security decisions automatically without prompting, which suits non-technical users. Bitdefender's engine is consistently one of the top two performers in independent tests.

**Best for:** Windows users who want comprehensive, reliable, low-maintenance protection.

### 2. Norton 360 Deluxe — Best for Identity Protection

Norton 360 Deluxe offers excellent malware protection paired with Norton's standout identity protection features. Dark web monitoring, LifeLock identity alerts, credit monitoring, and insurance for identity theft make it the best choice for users primarily concerned about identity theft.

It includes a full unlimited VPN — a meaningful differentiator over Bitdefender's limited free VPN tier. The 5-device coverage and cloud backup (50 GB) round out a comprehensive package.

Note: Norton's application can be resource-intensive on older machines.

**Best for:** Users who want strong antivirus combined with serious identity theft protection.

### 3. Malwarebytes Premium — Best for Malware Removal

Malwarebytes built its reputation as the best on-demand malware scanner — and Malwarebytes Premium extends that into real-time protection. It's particularly good at detecting and removing PUPs (potentially unwanted programs) and adware that some antivirus suites miss.

It's lighter on features than Bitdefender or Norton but is exceptionally effective at its core job. It pairs well with Windows Defender: Malwarebytes as a second-opinion scanner and for threat removal; Defender for baseline real-time protection.

**Best for:** Users who want a lean, effective anti-malware solution without feature bloat.

### 4. Kaspersky Plus — Best Bang for Buck

Kaspersky's detection rates are consistently among the highest of any antivirus vendor — regularly achieving 100% detection in independent tests. Kaspersky Plus includes an unlimited VPN, password manager, and identity monitoring at a competitive price.

Important context: Kaspersky is a Russian company, and the U.S. government issued a ban on Kaspersky software sales in 2024. For U.S. users, this may be a disqualifying factor regardless of technical merits. For users in other countries, Kaspersky Plus remains technically excellent.

**Best for:** Non-U.S. users who prioritize detection performance and value.

### 5. macOS: Intego Mac Internet Security X9 — Best for Mac

Many Mac users believe they don't need antivirus. They're wrong — Mac malware is growing in prevalence and sophistication. Intego is built specifically for macOS rather than ported from Windows, which makes it more effectively integrated with the platform.

Intego includes a two-way firewall (superior to macOS's built-in one-way firewall), anti-phishing, and scheduled scanning. Its Mac-specific malware detection is the best in the category.

**Best for:** Mac users who want Mac-native security software.

### 6. Windows Defender — Best Free Option

If you're running Windows 10 or 11 and keeping your system updated, Windows Defender provides solid baseline protection at no cost. It integrates seamlessly with the OS, has minimal performance impact, and its detection rates have improved dramatically in recent years.

Its weaknesses: it lacks some features of premium suites (VPN, identity monitoring, advanced ransomware recovery) and occasionally scores slightly lower in independent testing. But for budget-conscious users willing to practice good security hygiene, it's a meaningful and free starting point.

**Best for:** Users who want free, built-in protection and practice strong security habits.

## Mobile Antivirus: Do You Need It?

**Android:** Yes. Android allows sideloading (installing apps outside the Google Play Store), which creates meaningful risk. Reputable Android antivirus apps (Bitdefender, Norton, Malwarebytes) provide real value.

**iOS:** Less necessary. Apple's sandboxing and App Store review make iOS malware rare. iOS security apps primarily offer phishing protection, VPN, and identity monitoring rather than true antivirus scanning. Useful but not as critical as on Android.

## Red Flags in Antivirus Products

- **Excessive permissions** — Any antivirus asking for permissions beyond what's needed for security functions.
- **Aggressive upsell tactics** — Products that constantly push upgrades or scare users into purchases.
- **Poor independent test results** — Check AV-TEST and AV-Comparatives before buying. Marketing claims don't substitute for independent verification.
- **Free VPNs bundled with "free" antivirus** — Free products monetize your data in some other way.

## Final Verdict

- **Best overall:** Bitdefender Total Security — best balance of protection, performance, and features.
- **Best identity protection:** Norton 360 Deluxe — identity monitoring and LifeLock coverage stand out.
- **Best for Mac:** Intego Mac Internet Security X9 — built for macOS from the ground up.
- **Best free:** Windows Defender — solid baseline for Windows users committed to good security hygiene.
- **Best malware removal:** Malwarebytes Premium — the go-to for cleaning up infected systems.

Whatever you choose, install it, keep it updated, and let it run. The best antivirus is the one you actually use consistently.
